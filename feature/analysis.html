Rhythm

The time between keys inside words. A syllable is typed as one quick movement, with a small step into the next one; in Turkish tests the two are measured apart. Unevenness is how far a gap inside a syllable typically strays from your usual gap there, as a share of it: 0 % would be a metronome. A mistake counts as rushed when typed in under ¾ of your usual gap at that kind of spot, hesitant when over 1.5×.
